About Our Institute

Founded in 1951, the Global Research Institute has been at the forefront of interdisciplinary research for over seven decades. Our scholars collaborate with universities, governments, and civil society organisations worldwide to address the most pressing challenges of our time.

Our Mission

We are committed to producing rigorous, independent research that informs policy, advances human understanding, and contributes to a more equitable and sustainable world. We do not accept direction from funders on the conclusions of our work.

Core Values
  • Integrity and independence in all research activities
  • Openness and accessibility of knowledge
  • Collaboration across disciplines and national borders
  • Long-term commitment to societal impact

Research Areas

Climate & Environmental Policy

Our climate research team investigates the intersection of environmental science and public policy. Recent projects include a five-year longitudinal study on carbon pricing mechanisms in emerging economies and a comparative analysis of national adaptation strategies.

Our findings are submitted directly to legislative bodies across 40 countries. The team is currently advising three national governments on emissions reporting frameworks ahead of the 2026 review cycle.

Digital Governance & Technology Ethics

As artificial intelligence reshapes institutions and societies, our digital governance programme examines the accountability frameworks, regulatory approaches, and ethical standards needed to ensure that technological change serves the public interest.

The programme collaborates with technical universities, civil liberties organisations, and standards bodies to develop practical guidance for policymakers and technologists alike. Our 2025 white paper on algorithmic accountability has been cited in parliamentary committee proceedings in six countries.

Transparency & Funding

The Global Research Institute is committed to full transparency in its funding and operations. Our annual reports and financial statements are publicly available.

Our core funding comes from a consortium of European research foundations, supplemented by project-specific grants from national science agencies. We do not accept funding from commercial entities with a direct interest in our research outcomes. The Institute's endowment, established in 1965, provides approximately 30% of our annual operating budget. Full audited accounts for the financial year 2024–25 are available on request from our finance department at finance@global-research.example.org.
